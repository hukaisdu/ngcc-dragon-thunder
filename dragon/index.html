NGCC Round-1 hash candidate · hash-07

Dragon Hash Family

The 16-round ARX permutation Dragon-p on a 1600-bit state, wrapped in the sponge-forward mode. Six instances, KAT 6/6, and roughly 2.6× SHA3-512 in software at long messages.

Authors — Meiqin Wang, Kai Hu, Zhuolong Zhang, Yong Fu, Yanhong Fan, Lixuan Wu, Shiyao Chen, Jiamin Cui, Zhongfeng Niu, Qun Liu, Chenhao Jia, Longzheng Cui (Shandong University · Nanyang Technological University, Singapore).

TL;DR — Dragon = the 16-round ARX permutation Dragon-p in the sponge-forward mode; six instances (512/768/1024 + three XOFs). In software the whole family beats SHA3-512 at every tested length (Dragon-512 ≈ 2.6× at long messages); the first hardware synthesis closes at 251–258 MHz, so its ASIC throughput is currently below SHA3-512. Security targets up to 512-bit collision / 1024-bit preimage.
