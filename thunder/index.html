NGCC Round-1 hash candidate · hash-33

Thunder Hash Family

The 12-round permutation Thunder-p (Keccak-p lane geometry, degree-3 ψ) in the sponge-forward mode. Six instances, KAT 6/6, and up to +82% ASIC throughput over SHA3-512.

Authors — Meiqin Wang, Kai Hu, Hao Lei, Yanhong Fan, Lixuan Wu, Yong Fu, Shiyao Chen, Jiamin Cui, Zhongfeng Niu, Qun Liu, Chenhao Jia, Longzheng Cui (Shandong University · Nanyang Technological University, Singapore).

TL;DR — Thunder = the 12-round Keccak-p-shaped permutation Thunder-p (degree-3 layer ψ, double-parity θ) in the sponge-forward mode; six instances. In software Thunder-512/768 beat SHA3-512 at every tested length (512 ≈ 1.8×, ARM ≈ 2.0× at long messages); in hardware all three instances out-throughput SHA3-512 (up to +82% at 64 KiB). Security targets up to 512-bit collision / 1024-bit preimage.

permutationThunder-p: 12-round, 1600-bit (5×5×64) permutation using the Keccak-p lane geometry; round R = ψ ∘ π ∘ θ ∘ ρ ∘ ι (order ι→ρ→θ→π→ψ), with degree-3 ψ and double-column-parity θ
instancesThunder-512 / -768 / -1024 (fixed-output hash) and Thunder-XOF-256 / -384 / -512 (XOF)
